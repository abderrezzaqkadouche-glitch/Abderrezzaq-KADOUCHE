## 🔬 Case Studies

### 1.[Efficacy of Adjuvant Chemotherapy (5-FU + Levamisole) in Stage II/III Colon Cancer](colon-cancer-phase3-randomized-trial.html)

**Statistical Methods Used:** 
* Kaplan-Meier curves.
* Log-Rank test.
* Cox Proportional Hazards model.

### 2.[Longitudinal Evaluation of FEV1 Changes and Treatment Efficacy in Patients with Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease](fev1-copd.html)

**Statistical Methods Used:**
* Mixed Model for Repeated Measures (MMRM).

### 3.[Impact of Right Heart Catheterization on 30-Day Mortality in Critically Ill Patients: An Observational Cohort Study](rhc.html)

**Statistical Methods Used:**
* Propensity score and matching.
* Conditional logistic regression model.

### 4.[Prognostic Factors of Melanoma-Specific Mortality: A Retrospective Cohort](melanoma.html)

**Statistical Methods Used:**
* Competing Risk Analysis.
* Cumulative incidence functions (CIF).
* Gray’s test.
* Fine and Gray subdistribution hazard model.
  
### 5.[Clinical Predictors of Type 2 Diabetes](diabetes.html)

**Statistical Methods Used:**
* Multivariable Logistic Regression.
* Multiple Imputation for missing data.
* Complete-case analysis.
  
### 6.[Determinants of Plasma Retinol Concentrations: A Cross-Sectional Study](retinol.html)

**Statistical Methods Used:**
* Multiple Linear Regression.
* Chi-Square Test.
* Student test.
* ANOVA (Analysis of Variance).

### 7.[Analyzing Hospital Length of Stay: A Registry-Based Study](length-of-hospital-stay.html)

**Statistical Methods Used:**
* Negative Binomial Regression.
  
### 8.[Lung Cancer Cohort: A Survival Analysis](lung.html)

**Statistical Methods Used:** 
* Kaplan-Meier curves.
* Log-Rank test.
* Cox Proportional Hazards model.
* Multiple Imputation for missing data.
